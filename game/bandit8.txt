data.txt contains many lines, and the password is the only line that appears exactly once.

sort data.txt | uniq -u


why sort?
Because uniq only works properly when duplicate lines are next to each other

uniq -u

removes lines that have duplicates and leaves:
uniq detects adjacent duplicate lines, so sort is usually needed first when the duplicates aren't already grouped together.
The | is called a pipe. It lets you take the output of one command and send it directly into another command.
